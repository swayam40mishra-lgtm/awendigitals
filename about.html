Our story

Every business deserves to be discovered.

AWEN DIGITALS is a website development and creative digital-services initiative focused on helping small businesses establish a professional presence online without making the process complicated or expensive.

Many businesses already have useful services, great products, photographs and loyal customers. What they often lack is a dedicated place online that brings their information together and makes it easier for new customers to get in touch.

That's where AWEN comes in. We create affordable, mobile-friendly websites and creative digital content that help businesses present what they do clearly.

Our goal is simple: make getting online easier, more accessible and less intimidating for small business owners.
