FAQs

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Here are the basics. If your project has specific needs, we'll confirm the details in writing before starting.

What does a website starting at ₹299 include?

The intended starter offer is a simple, tightly scoped one-page website with agreed sections, a responsive layout and basic contact action where appropriate. The exact deliverables must be confirmed in a written quote. Extra pages and features cost more.

Why does the final website price vary?

Price depends on page count, layout complexity, content support, galleries, integrations, animations and custom features. We confirm scope and price before work begins.

Is a custom domain included?

Not by default. Domain purchase and renewal costs are separate unless your written quote explicitly includes them. A free subdomain may be an option for some projects.

Can a website use free hosting?

Potentially, depending on the site and the host's current limits, terms, custom-domain support and usage rules. We will explain the relevant trade-offs before you agree.

Who provides text and photos?

The customer supplies core business facts and usable photos. AWEN can help organise the wording, but the customer must approve factual details such as prices, phone numbers, services and opening hours.

How many revision rounds are included?

Three revision rounds are the selected website policy. A round means one consolidated set of reasonable changes to the agreed scope. New pages, new functionality or a complete redesign are not revisions.

How much does reel editing cost?

Reel editing starts at ₹199. The final quote depends on footage length, finished duration, complexity, captions, music, assets and turnaround. Exact inclusions are confirmed before work starts.

What does graphic design cost?

Graphic-design pricing is currently quote-based. We'll confirm asset type, quantity, dimensions, deliverables, revisions and source-file requirements before quoting.

How do payments and handover work?

The project scope and price are confirmed in writing. The agreed process should include draft review and revisions, with final payment completed before transfer of the final files, credentials or ownership where applicable.

Do you guarantee sales, rankings or viral views?

No. We focus on the agreed deliverables and quality of the process. Sales, search rankings, reach and social views depend on factors outside our full control.

Can you build booking systems or e-commerce?

Those features need a separate assessment and quote. They are not included in the entry-level website offer by default.

Do you provide support after launch?

Post-launch support, content updates and maintenance must be explicitly agreed. They are not automatically unlimited or lifelong.
