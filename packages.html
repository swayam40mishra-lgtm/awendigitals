Important details

What affects the final quote?

Page count, custom functionality, content readiness, image work, turnaround and third-party services all affect project effort.

  • Additional pages and features are priced separately.
  • Domain registration and renewal are separate unless explicitly included.
  • Free hosting/subdomains may have limits and aren't suitable for every project.
  • Three revision rounds apply only to the agreed scope; new features or a full redesign are not revisions.
  • Post-launch updates and ongoing maintenance are not included unless written into the quote.
  • Final payment and handover follow the terms confirmed in writing.
